BRICS 2024 SUMMIT

BRICS SUMMIT 2024 was held on October 22-24 Thursday in Southwestern city of Kazan, Russia. The summit was hosted by Russian president Vladimir Putin. The summit marks as the largest gathering of world leaders in Russia in decades and it happened at a time when the kremlin is locked in a war on western backed Ukraine is notable. In 2023, BRICS extended their invitations to Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates as these countries applied for membership. Other than Saudi Arabia all other countries have joined. Leaders of BRICS member countries – including Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Chinese President Xi Jinping and South African President Cyril Ramaphosa have attended the summit. Putin said more than 30 countries had expressed a desire to join the BRICS. He wanted to show that the western countries attempt to isolate Russia over Ukraine had failed. Under the theme "Strengthening Multilateralism for Just Global Development and Security," the summit addresses crucial issues that affect global stability. The Chinese president Xi Jinping and the Indian prime minister Narendra Modi met at the summit two days after agreeing to resolve four-year military stand-off on their Himalayan frontier. BRICS members share a common dissatisfaction with Western-led global governance, especially in economics. The BRICS nations are interested in creating a new currency to compete with the US dollar, and recently announced plans for a block chain-based payment system. The BRICS nations were widely expected to continue their discussions of creating a potentially gold-backed currency, known as the "Unit," as an alternative to the US dollar. The potential BRICS currency would allow these nations to assert their economic independence while competing with the existing international financial system. The sanctions on Russia after its 2022 invasion of Ukraine alarmed many Global South nations, fearing the West could weaponized financial tools against them. Russia and China have since capitalized on anti-Western sentiment, criticizing Western double standards and economic coercion. While middle powers don't seek to replace U.S. dominance with Chinese control, they are open to a more fragmented, autonomous world. BRICS aims to reduce reliance on the U.S. dollar and SWIFT, from which Russian banks were excluded in 2022. One of the major highlights of BRICS Summit 2024 is the adoption of the Kazan Declaration, which emphasizes solidarity among member countries and outlines key areas for cooperation, including sustainable development, reforms in global governance, and enhanced collaboration in trade and health initiatives.

DO YOU KNOW

Unlike many international organizations, BRICS does not have a permanent secretariat or headquarters. It operates through summits, meetings, and agreements made by consensus.
